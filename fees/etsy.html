FAQ

How much does Etsy take per sale?

For US sellers as of Oct 2026: Listing fee: $0.20; Transaction fee: 6.5%; Etsy Payments processing: 3% + $0.25; Offsite Ads fee (shop < $10k/365d): 15% (max $100) (optional); Offsite Ads fee (shop ≥ $10k/365d): 12% (max $100) (optional); Currency conversion fee (listing currency ≠ payment account currency): 2.5% (optional); Regulatory Operating fee (by seller country): 0%. Our worked example: on a $25.00 item with $5.00 shipping, estimated Etsy fees are $3.30 (11.0% of the buyer's total).

What does this Etsy fee calculator leave out?

It excludes sales tax on transaction fee (US), Etsy Ads, Share & Save, shipping labels.

Is my data saved?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser; nothing is stored or sent.

Is this tool made by Etsy?

No. Ledgerly Studio is independent and not affiliated with Etsy. Always confirm fees on Etsy's official pages.
