FAQ

How much does eBay take per sale?

For US sellers as of Oct 2026: Final value fee, most categories: 13.6% up to $7,500, 2.35% above; Final value fee, books/movies/music: 15.3% up to $7,500, 2.35% above; Final value fee, collectible card games / coins: 13.25% up to $7,500, 2.35% above; Per-order fee: $0.40 ($0.30 if order ≤ $10); International fee: 1.65% (optional). Our worked example: on a $25.00 item with $5.00 shipping, estimated eBay fees are $4.48 (14.9% of the buyer's total).

What does this eBay fee calculator leave out?

It excludes insertion fees beyond 250 free listings ($0.35), listing upgrades, promoted listings, Store subscription rates, below-standard surcharge, other category rates.

Is my data saved?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser; nothing is stored or sent.
